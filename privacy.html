Privacy Policy

Effective: August 31, 2026 · Currently available in English only.

Who we are

Ung Kheang Heang Dental Clinic ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and appointment booking system for our clinic in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. This policy explains what information we collect through this site, why, and how it's protected.

Information we collect

When you request an appointment

  • Full name and phone number (required), and email address (optional)
  • Patient type (new or returning), the service you're requesting, and your preferred doctor, date, and time
  • An optional note about your visit, and a medical condition category (e.g. high blood pressure, diabetes, allergies, or none) if you choose to share one, along with any description you provide
  • Your consent to be contacted about the request, which is required to submit the form

When you send us a message

  • Your full name, email address, and the message you write

Automatically, for security

  • A cryptographically hashed version of your IP address, used only to detect spam and limit abuse. We never store your raw IP address, and the hash cannot be reversed to reveal it.
  • A verification token from Cloudflare Turnstile, used to confirm you're a real visitor rather than an automated bot. Turnstile's own data handling is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not run analytics or marketing pixels on this site.

How we use this information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Review and respond to your appointment request or message
  • Contact you to confirm, reschedule, or discuss your visit
  • Maintain a record of your appointment history with our clinic

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who can see your information

Only authenticated clinic staff can view appointment requests and contact messages, and only after signing in through our staff system. Database-level security rules (Row Level Security) restrict this data so that no one else — including anyone without a staff login — can access it directly.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored using Supabase (a managed database provider) and served through Cloudflare. Both are third-party infrastructure providers who process this data on our behalf under their own security commitments; neither uses your information for their own purposes.

How long we keep your information

We currently keep appointment requests and contact messages indefinitely, as a record of clinic activity, unless you ask us to delete yours (see "Your choices" below).

Booking on behalf of someone else

You may submit an appointment request on behalf of a child or family member. Please only do so with their knowledge, and avoid including sensitive details in the free-text message field beyond what's needed to book the visit.

Your choices

  • The email field on the booking form is optional — you may leave it blank.
  • The medical condition field is required, but you can select "None" if it doesn't apply to you or you'd rather discuss it with us in person.
  • To request a copy of your information, or to ask us to delete it, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our practices change. The effective date at the top will reflect the most recent update.
